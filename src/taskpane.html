Select shapes on the slide, then use a shortcut. With one shape selected, align shortcuts snap it to the slide edge or center. With 2+ shapes, align shortcuts match shapes to each other. Group by row/column needs 2+ shapes. Distribute needs 3+ shapes. Margin shortcuts need the shape border selected (not inside the text). Cmd+Z undoes any action.
